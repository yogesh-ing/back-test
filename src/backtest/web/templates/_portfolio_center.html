🛑 Modify Stop-Loss

The stop fires on the next mark at or below this level and market-exits the position. It is checked on every bar — the strategy does not have to trade for it to work.

🎯 Modify Target

The target fires on the next mark at or above this level, booking the position out at market. For an option structure the level is the net premium per unit (the same number the Current column shows).

📉 Close Part of Position

Scaling out books the realised P&L on the closed part and leaves the rest working with its stop and target intact.

✎ Amend Working Order

The venue is asked first — if it refuses, nothing changes here. An amended order keeps its client order id, so filled quantity, slippage and the audit trail continue on the same order instead of starting a new one.

🚪 Close Position

This market-exits the position now at the last price and books the realised P&L. Option structures close every leg together.

The strategy may re-enter on the next bar if it still wants the trade — pause the runner if you want it to stand down.

🔴 Emergency Flatten All

This will cancel new entries and market-exit every open position across all runners, then halt the portfolio.

Use only for risk emergencies. This cannot be undone.
