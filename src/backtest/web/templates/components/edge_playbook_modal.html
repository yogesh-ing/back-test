How Strategy Analytics (`/analytics`) and the PnL Diary (`/diary`) work hand-in-hand to diagnose edge, cut friction, and graduate strategies safely.

Short answer: They are not duplicates — they work hand-in-hand as the two halves of a prop-trading desk review. Analytics (`/analytics`) is your Macro Time-Series & Broker Execution Radar (detecting when Sharpe degrades or which broker adds slippage). PnL Diary (`/diary`) is your Micro-Attribution, Intraday Regime Microscope & Graduation Gatekeeper (isolating why a runner bled, which parameter fixes it, and whether it passes all 6 gates for Live capital).

Run this 5-Step Diagnostic Loop after every 10–20 closed Paper trades (or weekly on Live runners). Change one parameter lever at a time and spawn a new Paper instance so before-vs-after configs remain cleanly comparable.

Step 1 · Isolate Strategy Family vs Spawned Instance (`/diary` → Attribution)

Granularity Check

Expand each strategy row into its individual Spawned Instances (`symbol · timeframe · SL/TP/trail · capital`).

  • Common finding: Strategy rollup looks mediocre (`PF = 1.08×`) because one illiquid symbol or `1min` spawn (`PF = 0.65×`) is dragging down a `15min` NIFTY spawn (`PF = 1.82×`).
  • Action: Retire the losing instance (`Stop` on `/portfolio/paper`) instead of rewriting core strategy signal code.

Step 2 · Audit the Exit-Reason Attribution Matrix (`/diary` → Regimes → Matrix A)

Exit Surgery
  • `signal` (Signal Flip) bleeding while `take_profit` is green: Indicator flips in sideways chop, closing winners prematurely and crystallizing whipsaws. Fix: disable `signal_flip` exit (or require confirmation bars) and rely on `stop_loss` / `take_profit` / `trailing_stop`.
  • `stop_loss` > 65% of exits with short hold duration: Stop sits inside normal 1-bar ATR noise. Fix: widen SL to `1.5×–2.0× ATR` and reduce quantity so rupee risk stays constant.
  • `trailing_stop` Avg Win < `|Avg Loss|`: Trail arms too early and chokes trend runners. Fix: raise trail activation threshold before ratcheting.
  • `eod_flat` (`15:15–15:30` square-off) consistently negative: Late entries run out of session clock. Fix: block fresh intraday entries after `14:30 IST`.

Step 3 · Filter NSE Intraday IST Microstructure Regimes (`/diary` → Regimes → Matrix B)

Switch Clock = Entry Time (IST)
  • `09:15–09:45` Opening Drive: Wide spreads & overnight order-imbalance discovery. Mean-reversion fades often get steamrolled; wait until `09:30–09:45 IST` unless running dedicated opening-range breakouts.
  • `09:45–11:30` Morning Trend: Highest institutional directional follow-through on NSE. Concentrate trend/momentum risk here.
  • `11:30–13:30` Midday Chop: Volume trough & rangebound noise. Trend strategies frequently give back 60–80% of morning gains here — add a midday entry blackout if `Entry Clock` Net P&L is negative.
  • `13:30–15:10` Afternoon / Gamma & `15:10–15:30` Square-Off: European overlap + expiry gamma pin/acceleration; avoid opening new intraday positions inside `15:10–15:30 IST`.

Step 4 · Eliminate Sub-Minute Churn & Clear the Breakeven Curve (`/diary` → Payoff & Drag)

Friction Control
  • Inspect Hold-Duration Buckets (`< 1m` Churn Watch, `1–5m` Scalp, `5–15m`, `15–60m`, `60m+`). If `< 1m` or `1–5m` shows `Drag > 50%`, step up from `1min` to `5min`/`15min` bars or add a trade cooldown.
  • Check `Avg Commission / Trade` (**Fee Hurdle**): your target move must be at least `3×–4×` this rupee hurdle.
  • Inspect the Payoff Quadrant: move bubbles right (higher `W` via stricter entry regime filters) or up (higher `R` via wider TP / tighter SL) until well above `R = (1 − W) / W`.

Step 5 · Cross-Check `/analytics` & Execute 6-Gate Graduation (`/diary` → Graduation & Journal)

Capital Governance
  • Confirm `6/6` gates green (`PROMOTE_CANDIDATE`) in `/diary`, verify `entitlement_advisory` for option structures, and check `/analytics` for smooth rolling Sharpe + clean broker fills.
  • Record every parameter change or market anomaly in the Operator Session Journal (`trend_day`, `chop_whipsaw`, `expiry_gamma`, `slippage_friction`, `parameter_tuning`, `risk_discipline`).

1. Single-Sum Commission Rule (`E2`)2dp Exact

Net P&L = Gross P&L − Commission

Operator Scope Guard: `Commission` is always displayed and reconciled as one single combined sum (brokerage + statutory charges). Every trade row, cell, and KPI card is formatted to exact `2` decimal places (`tabular-nums`) and verified within `±₹0.02`.

2. Net Expectancy & Breakeven HurdlePayoff Math

E[net] = (W × AvgWin_net) − ((1 − W) × |AvgLoss_net|)
R = AvgWin_net / |AvgLoss_net|  ·  R_BE = (1 − W) / W

`W` is after-cost Win Rate (`Net P&L > 0`). Whenever `R > R_BE`, `E[net]` is strictly positive; the vertical distance `R − R_BE` is your payoff safety cushion.

Net Win Rate (`W`) Breakeven Payoff (`R_BE = (1−W)/W`) Target Payoff for `PF ≈ 1.50×` Archetype Strategy Profile
30.0% 2.33× 3.50× Long-option breakout / convex trend follower (many small premium stops, occasional 4×–6× runners).
40.0% 1.50× 2.25× Intraday momentum swing (`5m–15m` bars with ATR stop + trailing profit).
50.0% 1.00× 1.50× Balanced directional intraday / multi-leg debit spread (`bull_call_spread`, `bear_put_spread`).
60.0% 0.67× 1.00× Mean-reversion / VWAP pullback with disciplined hard stop-loss.
70.0% 0.43× 0.64× High-probability theta / range capture — watch tail `Max DD ≤ 10%` closely against 1-day gap events.
